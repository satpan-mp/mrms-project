# MRMS Backlogs

> **Purpose:** Separate functional (product) work from technical work and track debt, infrastructure, and future ideas.
> **Scope:** All planned work not yet scheduled into an active sprint task.
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Author:** MRMS Engineering Team - PT Mitra Prodin
> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-20
> **Related Documents:** [Development Roadmap](../18-Development-Roadmap.md), [Sprint Planning](../19-Sprint-Planning.md), [FRS](../02-FRS-Functional-Requirement-Specification.md), [Risk Register](../RISK-REGISTER.md), [Definition of Ready](../process/definition-of-ready.md)
> **References:** -

These backlogs are the planning source that feeds sprints. Items move from a
backlog into a sprint only after they satisfy the
[Definition of Ready](../process/definition-of-ready.md), and are considered
finished only when they meet the [Definition of Done](../process/definition-of-done.md).

| Backlog | Contents |
|---------|----------|
| [Product Backlog](./product-backlog.md) | User-facing features and requirements (traces to FRS) |
| [Technical Backlog](./technical-backlog.md) | Engineering enablers, non-feature work |
| [Technical Debt](./technical-debt.md) | Known shortcuts to repay; includes init-phase debt |
| [Infrastructure Backlog](./infrastructure-backlog.md) | Deployment, CI/CD, observability, ops |
| [Future Improvements](./future-improvements.md) | Roadmap / post-v1 ideas |

## Item ID scheme

- Product: `PB-NNN`  ·  Technical: `TB-NNN`  ·  Debt: `TD-NNN`  ·
  Infrastructure: `IB-NNN`  ·  Future: `FI-NNN`

## Priority & estimate

Priority: High / Medium / Low. Estimates are indicative story points, refined at
sprint planning (see [Sprint Planning](../19-Sprint-Planning.md)).

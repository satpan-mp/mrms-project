# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the Meeting Room Management
System (MRMS) pledge to make participation in our project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or
experience level.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the team.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers at PT Mitra Prodin. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
